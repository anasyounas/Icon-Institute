Where the project stands

Every function promised in the Technical Review Response has been built. The content management system is complete and working: your team can add and edit news, jobs, projects, page text, images, documents and search-engine details, and what they publish appears on the website.

How to review it

Once the redeploy is done, sign in to the CMS at /admin/login with the administrator account. You will be asked to choose your own password the first time — that step is deliberate, and afterwards you can switch on two-factor sign-in from Security & 2FA, which shows a QR code to scan with an authenticator app.

A five-minute test of the whole chain

  1. Open News Manager and choose Add news article. Fill in a headline and date, pick an image, and save the draft.
  2. Confirm it is not on the public news page yet — drafts stay private.
  3. Press Publish, then reload the public news page. The article is there.
  4. Edit the headline and reload again to see the change carry through.
  5. Press Archive and confirm it disappears from the site.

The same pattern applies to jobs, projects, page text, contact details and search-engine settings.
